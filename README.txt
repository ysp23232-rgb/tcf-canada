TCF CANADA OFFLINE PRACTICE

Open index.html in Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox.

Everything needed for the 30 listening exams and 40 reading exams is stored inside this folder. Internet access is not required. Listening audio continues uninterrupted while you move between questions. Progress and completed scores are saved automatically in your browser on this device.

After submitting, the review shows separate Correct, Incorrect, and Skipped totals, your percentage, your answer, the correct answer, and the full source explanation or listening transcription for every question.

LEARNING MODE
Choose "Learn & review" on any exam card. There is no timer and no need to submit. Select an answer to see immediately whether it is correct, or use "Reveal answer and explanation." The correct answer, your answer, and the full explanation/transcription appear on the same question. Listening audio continues while you navigate.

WRITING AND SPEAKING
Use the Expression écrite and Expression orale tabs to open the lessons as real offline webpages—not PDF viewers or page screenshots. The app includes all 3 writing tasks with 385 subjects and corrections, plus all 7 speaking sections. Use the left-side lesson navigation to move between sections. Headings, objectives, highlighted material, subjects, corrections, and source illustrations are organized in the same course-style format as the reference site.

TCF CANADA STUDY GUIDES
Expression écrite includes three separate Task 1, Task 2, and Task 3 writing guides. Expression orale includes three separate TCF Canada speaking guides: the guided interview, role-play interaction, and expressing/defending a point of view. The speaking guides include the official-style format, timing, preparation method, evaluation priorities, answer structures, useful expressions, examples, common mistakes, and practice strategies. All six guides work offline.

Keep this entire folder together. Do not move index.html away from the media, data, and style files.
